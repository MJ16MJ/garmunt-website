Frequently Asked Questions

We accept all major file formats including PNG, JPG, SVG, AI (Adobe Illustrator), EPS, and PDF. For the best print quality, we recommend vector formats such as .AI, .EPS, or .SVG. If you only have a low-resolution image, our design team can often recreate it for you at no extra charge.

For screen printing, the minimum order is 10 items per design. Embroidery and DTG printing have no minimum — you can order from just 1 item. This makes embroidery and DTG ideal for samples, one-offs, or small team orders.

Screen printing and embroidery orders typically ship within 3-5 working days after proof approval. DTG printing takes 5-7 working days. Rush orders are available for an additional fee — just let us know your deadline and we will do our best to meet it.

Absolutely. Every order receives a free digital proof showing exactly how your design will look on the garment. We will not start production until you have approved the proof. Revisions are unlimited and free of charge.

Yes, we offer full Pantone colour matching for screen printing and embroidery at no extra cost. Simply provide your Pantone reference(s) when placing your order. For DTG printing, colours are matched as closely as possible using CMYK profiles.
