Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ordering, delivery, customisation and more.

You can place an order directly through our website by browsing our catalogue, adding items to your basket, and proceeding to checkout. If you have a trade account, log in first to see your discounted pricing. For large or custom orders, you can also contact our sales team on 0800 000 0000 or email hello@garmunt.com and we'll put together a quote for you.

There is no minimum order quantity for blank garments -- you can order as little as one item. For printed or embroidered garments, the minimum order is 10 units per design. This allows us to set up the print process efficiently while keeping costs competitive.

Yes, single samples are available on all blank garments at standard pricing. Simply add the item to your basket and check out as normal. If you need a printed sample, contact our sales team and we can arrange a sample run for you -- a small set-up fee may apply depending on the print method.

Yes, we offer trade accounts with exclusive wholesale pricing. You can apply online through our website -- the process takes just a few minutes. Once approved, you'll have access to discounted trade prices across our entire product range. Trade accounts also benefit from extended payment terms and a dedicated account manager.

Standard delivery takes 3-5 working days. Express next-day delivery is available on orders placed before 3pm (Monday to Friday). Custom-printed orders require an additional 3-5 working days for production before dispatch. You'll receive tracking information via email once your order has been shipped.

Yes, we offer free standard delivery on all orders over £150 (ex. VAT) within mainland UK. For orders under £150, standard delivery is charged at a flat rate of £5.95. Express and international shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.

Yes, we offer global freight and international delivery to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. For large international orders, contact our logistics team for a bespoke shipping quote. We ship via FedEx and DHL for international orders to ensure reliable, tracked delivery.

We use DPD for express and next-day deliveries within the UK, Royal Mail for standard and smaller parcels, and FedEx for international shipments. All deliveries include full tracking so you can monitor your order from dispatch to doorstep. You'll receive your tracking details by email as soon as your order ships.

We offer a full range of customisation methods including screen printing (ideal for large runs), embroidery (perfect for logos and workwear), and DTG (direct-to-garment) printing for photographic or complex multi-colour designs. We also offer heat transfers and vinyl printing for smaller quantities. Our team can advise on the best method for your project.

For best results, we accept vector files in AI, EPS, or PDF format. For DTG and digital printing, we also accept high-resolution PNG files (300dpi or higher). If you don't have artwork in these formats, our in-house design team can help convert or recreate your logo -- just get in touch and we'll provide a quote for artwork services.

Yes, absolutely. We provide a digital proof within 24 hours of receiving your artwork and order details. You'll be able to review the placement, sizing, and colours before we go to production. We won't start printing until you've approved the proof, so you can be confident in the final result.

Customisation typically takes 3-5 additional working days on top of standard delivery times. This includes proof approval, setup, and production. For urgent orders, we offer an express customisation service -- contact our team to discuss turnaround options. Turnaround times may vary for very large orders (1,000+ units).

We accept returns on blank (unprinted, unembroidered) garments within 14 days of delivery, provided items are unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging. Please note that customised items (printed or embroidered) cannot be returned unless they are faulty or incorrect. For full details, see our Returns Policy page.

To arrange a return, contact our customer service team by emailing hello@garmunt.com or calling 0800 000 0000. Please have your order number ready. We'll provide you with a returns authorisation number and instructions for sending the items back. Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer unless the item is faulty.

Yes, we offer a full refund on eligible returns within 14 days of delivery. Once we receive and inspect the returned items, your refund will be processed within 5-7 working days back to your original payment method. For faulty or incorrect items, we'll also cover the return shipping cost and can arrange a replacement or refund -- whichever you prefer.
