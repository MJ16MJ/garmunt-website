Product Description

The Portwest Hi-Vis Executive Vest is a professional-grade safety vest designed for construction teams, warehouse staff, event crews, and outdoor workers. Made from high-quality polyester with reflective tape for maximum visibility in low-light conditions.

Certified to EN ISO 20471 Class 2, this vest offers reliable compliance with UK workplace safety standards. Lightweight, breathable, and comfortable for all-day wear, it's available in multiple high-visibility colours and sizes.

Key Features

  • EN ISO 20471 Class 2 certified
  • 50mm retro-reflective tape across chest and back
  • Front zip fastening with heavy-duty ID pocket
  • Multiple storage pockets including radio loop
  • Machine washable up to 60°C
  • Perfect for screen printing and embroidery branding
