About Garmunt

Your trusted UK partner for blank apparel, workwear and custom garment printing.

Our Story

From Humble Beginnings to the UK's Trusted Apparel Partner

Founded in Manchester, Garmunt started with a simple mission: make high-quality blank apparel accessible to businesses of all sizes across the UK. What began as a small warehouse operation has grown into one of the country's most trusted suppliers of wholesale clothing, workwear, and custom-printed garments.

Our founders saw a gap in the market for a supplier that combined competitive trade pricing with genuinely fast turnaround times and a no-minimum-order policy. Over the years, we've built partnerships with over 100 leading brands and developed in-house printing and embroidery capabilities that let us handle everything from single samples to bulk runs of 10,000+ units.

Today, we serve over 50,000 customers nationwide -- from independent print shops and start-up clothing brands to large corporations and public-sector organisations. Our commitment remains the same: deliver quality garments, on time, at the best possible price.

What We Do

Our Services

Wholesale Distribution

Access over 4,000 products from 100+ leading brands at competitive trade prices. No minimum order quantities on blank garments, with next-day delivery available across the UK.

Custom Printing & Embroidery

Full in-house customisation services including screen printing, DTG (direct-to-garment), embroidery, and heat transfers. Digital proofs within 24 hours and production in 3-5 working days.

Warehousing & Fulfilment

Let us handle the logistics. We offer warehousing, pick-and-pack, and direct-to-customer fulfilment services. Ideal for e-commerce brands and businesses that want to scale without the overhead.
